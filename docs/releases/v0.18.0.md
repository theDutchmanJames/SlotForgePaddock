# SlotForge Paddock v0.18.0

Collection planning, a clearer workshop and more ways to enjoy your cars.

## Before updating

**Create a complete database backup in your current version first.** This release migrates saved collections to **schema 10**. Older v0.17.x builds cannot reopen those saves; keep your pre-update backup if you need to go back. The Timing v1 API contract is unchanged.

## What's new

- **Stock and purchase planning:** separate My stock, Catalogue and Shopping list views; link purchases to workshop jobs and receive all or part of an order into owned stock.
- **Workshop plans:** priorities, target dates, waiting status, checklists and before/after photos. A redesigned plan panel separates progress, purchases and photos, with clear empty states and a layout that adapts to narrower windows.
- **Setup comparisons:** compare saved setups with each other or with the car's current configuration, including tracked parts and quantities.
- **Printable PDF reports:** car cards with QR links, setup sheets, scrutineering sheets and collection inventory reports, with optional purchase information.
- **Showroom shelves:** organise cars into ordered shelves, adjust photo framing and use the clean presentation view.
- **Timing-compatible car exports:** choose the Timing JSON format when exporting cars for import into SlotForge Timing as new copies.
- **Custom connection passwords:** choose separate passwords for browse, submission and Timing access during a hosting session.
- **Better catalogue templates:** explicit English-language instructions and clearer classification guidance for AI-assisted manufacturer lists.
- **Source-available licence:** the repository now uses the SlotForge Source-Available License 1.0, matching Timing. See LICENSE for the terms.

## Fixes and polish

- Fixed Add shopping item failing on the standalone Shopping list page; adding from a job still preselects that job.
- Catalogue folders now use a blue accent, distinct from owned inventory's amber accent.
- Rapid checklist changes save in sequence without losing updates.
- Prevented shopping orders that cannot be received into individual or assembly-dedicated stock.
- Corrected current versus historical part names in comparisons and reports, and clarified unknown inventory in imported setups.
- Fixed photo framing identity collisions, tightened planning validation and corrected PDF footer/pagination behaviour.

## Downloads

Download **SlotForge.Paddock.0.18.0.exe**, close the previous copy, then open the new portable Windows app. It requires no installer or Node.js. The executable is unsigned. **SHA256SUMS.txt** covers the executable and the included **SlotForge-Paddock-Timing-v1.zip** integration guide, schemas and fixtures.

Linux/macOS installer builders are included in the source. Native binaries are not supplied in this release; native platform testing and physical two-computer Wi-Fi acceptance testing remain outstanding.
